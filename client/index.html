HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Throw right-click or SPACE
Boost hold left-click or SHIFT
Touch
Move left stick
Throw tap THROW: along your movement

Boost hold BOOST

Blades

  • Your blades orbit around you and clash with enemy blades. Each rarity deals and withstands the same amount: Common 1, Rare 2, Epic 4, Legendary 8. Equal rarities break each other; a Legendary survives 7 Common hits.
  • A blade touching a player's body kills. Your orbit is your shield: keep it full.
  • Grab loose blades to grow. At 10, 20, 35, 55 and 80 blades you tier up: a new blade shape (dagger, sword, scythe, saw, runic blade, aura) and, up to the scythe, a bigger reach and a harder knockback.

Throw and boost

  • Throwing launches your outermost blade (0.5 s cooldown). It can hit 1 target, 2 if Epic, 3 if Legendary, and lands after 30 u where anyone can pick it up.
  • Boost makes you 70 % faster but burns 1 blade every 0.5 s, cheapest first.

Arena

  • The red edge kills instantly, outer blades first. A red glow and an alarm warn you.
  • You spawn away from bigger players, near loose blades. For 10 s, bots leave you alone, then for 40 s they only go after you up close. This ends as soon as you throw or your blades hit someone.
  • Bushes hide you and your blades from other players.
  • Break crates for loot. Power-ups: Speed, Spin, Magnet, Shield, Blades.
  • When you die, 70 % of your blades drop, rarest first, plus those you lost in the last 10 s.
  • The leader wears a crown with a bounty: take them down for bonus trophies and all of their blades. Killing a player with at least twice your blades pays double.
  • Bots are marked BOT in the leaderboard. Some are easy, some hard; in your first game, they go easy on you.
  • Play with friends: INVITE A FRIEND (settings, in game) or SHARE on the end-of-life card sends a link to your arena. In a private room, tap the ROOM badge.
  • Chat: Enter, or the bubble on mobile. Commands: /me, /mute and /unmute, /report, /help. Insults are masked; three in two minutes and you are muted for two minutes.

Trophies

  • Earned at the end of each life in public rooms: kills × 15, your peak blade count, 1 per 10 s alive, crates × 3, power-ups × 2. Spend them in the BOUTIQUE.
  • Leaderboards of the day, the week, the season and all time, public games only. A season lasts six weeks: at its end, the top ten earn trophies (1,000 for the first, 750, 500, then 250).
  • Every day, three challenges (easy, medium, hard), and one per week: the same for everyone, renewed at midnight Paris time. Your lives in public rooms count; rewards are trophies. Find them in PROFILE.
  • They are also your XP: spending them does not lower your level, shown next to your name. New titles at levels 5, 10, 20, 30, 50, 75 and 100.
  • Cosmetics: skins, blade styles, trails and elimination effects, seen by everyone. Unlock them by leveling up (levels 2 to 30) or buy them in the BOUTIQUE, where three are 20% off each day (FEATURED) and every item can be tried on in 3D. They never change hitboxes, rarity colors or the rings on the ground.
